# Outline — executable tariff law

Section jobs + fact slots (keys → facts/ground-truth.md). First
sentences carry the most weight; each section's is drafted at writing
time, not here.

## Abstract
Job: the whole claim in one paragraph — 2025–26 made statutory tariff
rates a moving target; we encode the stack as dated, instrument-cited
executable rules; conformance-test it against the leading independent
tracker to unexplained-zero; find and report tracker defects with legal
derivations; and release synthetic transaction-level entries that
reproduce official margins exactly. Exemplar written: drafts/abstract.md.

## 1. Why 2026 broke tariff lookup
Job: establish that "the statutory rate on date X" became a research
problem. Slots: legal-timeline facts (IEEPA legal-vs-collection end,
§122 expiry, forced-labor determinations, HTS revision churn). Hook is
the two-date termination: a rate can be legally dead and still
collected. No advocacy — volatility stated as measurement burden.

## 2. What a tariff change is, formally
Job: the representation. Slots: encoding-mechanics facts (dated
versions, terminations as encoded law with the EO 14389 example, proof
atoms → corpus citations with excerpts, revision-consolidation modules,
composed spine). The claim: history is the representation; a change
cannot be encoded before its instrument is ingested, and cannot merge
without a boundary test.

## 3. Conformance against an independent tracker
Job: the validation methodology + result. Slots: conformance-result
facts (predicate, spine-generated universe, 10/10 / unexplained 0 @
dc1da3af, tripwired exclusions, temporal debt surfaced), corpus
grounding (138 versions), adversarial arc (r2→r7, 2,396 battery),
build-envelope measurement. Honest boundary: internal cross-model
review, not external certification (named future work).

## 4. When the oracle disagrees: dispositions with legal derivations
Job: the "oracles find bugs in the reference" mechanism. Slots: Yale
defect facts (#27 §201 staged-table end; #28 GN3(b)/column-2), the
disposition standard (evidence-backed derivation OR maintainer
acceptance; these have the former, await the latter — stated exactly),
and the cooperative posture (six upstream filings incl. the
publish-the-panel proposal #26). Framing gate: Max approves this
section's tone before anything outward.

## 5. Synthetic customs entries from official margins
Job: the dataset descriptor core. Slots: entries facts (bulk-file
sourcing doctrine + 18 sha-manifested archives; 38.8M rows → 3.07M
cells; exact-integer reconciliation ×3 axes, 0 failures ×18 months;
dual-channel cross-check 108,977 cells 0 mismatches; generator
exactness $1,467,109,590,515 pilot identity; assumptions register with
the σ proxy-moment disclosure; FYTD honesty machinery). The estimation/
law boundary paragraph lives here.

## 6. Provenance engineering (methods)
Job: what it takes for every number above to be checkable — and the
plain statement that AI agents built it behind gates. Slots: signed
corpus manifests; per-row digest bindings; byte-determinism; the
12-round adversarial review arc with mutant-proven closures (149-test
battery); windowless publication + the durability-boundary invariant as
a worked example of review convergence; snapshot-pinned scoreboards.

## 7. Uses and limits
Job: what the pair enables now (entry-level duty computation composed
with the statutory spine; calibration targets) and what it does not
claim (no effective rates, no incidence, no behavioral response — the
wedge analyses are downstream work). Every scope carve-out volunteered.

## 8. Related work
